AI myths vs facts

Myth: AI will take every job

Fact: AI changes tasks first. Some jobs shrink, new ones appear, and people who learn to use AI tools often become more valuable.

Myth: AI always tells the truth

Fact: AI can make mistakes and sound confident. Important answers about health, money or law should always be checked.

Myth: AI thinks like a human

Fact: AI finds patterns in data. It does not have feelings or real understanding of the world like a person does.

Myth: Only big companies can use AI

Fact: Students, small shops and farmers use free AI tools every day.
