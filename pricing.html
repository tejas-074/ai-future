Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. You can cancel from your account page and you will not be charged again.

Is my data safe?

Your data is encrypted and never sold. You can delete it whenever you want.

Do you offer student discounts?

Yes, students get 50% off the Pro plan. Contact us with your student email.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time and the price adjusts automatically.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept cards, UPI, net banking and major wallets. All payments are processed securely.

Is there a free trial for Pro?

Yes. Pro and Team include a 14-day free trial. You can cancel before it ends and pay nothing.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data stays for 30 days so you can download it. After that it is deleted permanently.

Do you offer invoices for companies?

Yes. Team plans include GST-ready invoices with your company name and tax number.
